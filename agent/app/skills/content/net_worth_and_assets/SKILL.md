---
name: net_worth_and_assets
description: Understand net worth and asset allocation - what the user owns, liquidity, concentration, how it changed.
---
## When to use
"What's my net worth?", "How are my assets split?", "Am I too concentrated in one thing?"

## Approach
1. Total assets from user data; subtract liabilities if present. If liabilities are missing, state that net worth excludes them.
2. Show allocation by asset type as percentages; mark liquid (cash, savings) vs illiquid (property, long-term holdings).
3. Flag concentration (one asset above ~50%) and low liquidity versus essential spending.
4. Suggest rebalancing ideas in general terms (diversify, raise liquidity), never specific securities.

## Output
Net worth line, allocation list, 1-2 observations.
