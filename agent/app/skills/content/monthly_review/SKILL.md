---
name: monthly_review
description: Summarise a month or period - income, spending, savings rate, budget results, goal progress, highlights and one focus for next month.
---
## When to use
"Give me my monthly summary", "How did I do this month?", "Review my finances."

## Approach
1. Income, total spending, net saved, and savings rate = net / income.
2. Top 3 categories, biggest change vs last period, budgets exceeded.
3. Goal progress since last period and upcoming bills.
4. One win to celebrate, one issue to fix, one focus for next month.
5. Keep numbers exact from the data; skip any section where data is missing.

## Output
A tidy summary with those sections in under 150 words plus the three closing points.
