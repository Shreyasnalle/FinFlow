description: Explain money concepts simply - inflation, interest, credit score, insurance, compound interest, loans, assets vs liabilities.
---
## When to use
"What is inflation?", "How does credit score work?", "Do I need insurance?", any "explain" money question.

## Approach
1. Explain in two or three plain sentences, no jargon, then one everyday example.
2. Tie it back to the user with one number from their data when it helps.
3. Keep it neutral and educational; do not recommend specific products.
4. Offer a related next concept only if it is natural.

## Output
Short explanation, one example, optional personal link.
