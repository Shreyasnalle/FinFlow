---
name: bills_and_subscriptions
description: Review recurring bills and subscriptions - upcoming dues, total fixed costs, what to cancel or renegotiate, avoiding late fees.
---
## When to use
"What bills are due?", "How much do I pay in subscriptions?", "Which should I cancel?", late fees.

## Approach
1. List recurring bills from user data with amount and due date; order by next due date.
2. Total the monthly fixed cost and show it as a share of income.
3. Flag the nearest dues (next 7 days) and any that look duplicated or low-value.
4. Suggest cancelling, downgrading or renegotiating specific items, with the yearly saving (monthly x 12).
5. If cash balance is lower than bills due soon, warn clearly.

## Output
Upcoming list, total fixed cost and share of income, then up to 3 actions.
