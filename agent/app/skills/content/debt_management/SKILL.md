## When to use
"How do I clear my loan?", "Which debt first?", "Is it better to prepay?", credit card balances, EMIs.

## Approach
1. Collect each debt: balance, interest rate, minimum payment. If missing, ask for it; do not invent.
2. Explain both strategies briefly: avalanche (highest rate first, least interest) and snowball (smallest balance first, quick wins). Recommend avalanche unless motivation is the concern.
3. Pay minimums on all, extra money to the target debt. Estimate months to be debt-free and interest saved vs minimum-only, with a simple calculation.
4. Compare prepaying a loan against saving only after the emergency fund exists; high-rate debt (>10-12%) usually beats saving.
5. Use web_search only for current lending rates or product terms, and name the source.

## Output
Recommended order, extra payment needed, estimated payoff time. Never recommend taking new debt to invest.
