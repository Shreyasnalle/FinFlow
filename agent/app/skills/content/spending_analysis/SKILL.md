---
name: spending_analysis
description: Explain where the user's money goes - category breakdown, trends, biggest expenses, unusual spikes, where to cut.
---
## When to use
"Where is my money going?", "Why did I overspend?", "What are my top expenses?", comparisons between months.

## Approach
1. From the user data, rank categories by spend this period and give each as a share of total spending.
2. Compare with the previous period or with budgets if present; call out the 1-3 biggest movers.
3. Separate essentials (rent, utilities, groceries, transport) from discretionary (eating out, shopping, entertainment).
4. Flag one-off spikes versus recurring drift.
5. Suggest at most 3 specific, realistic cuts with an estimated monthly saving each.

## Output
A short ranked list, one insight sentence, then the 1-3 suggestions. No lecturing.

## Watch out
Never guess missing categories. If there are few transactions, say the picture is incomplete.
