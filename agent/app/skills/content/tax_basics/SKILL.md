## When to use
"How much tax will I pay?", "How can I save tax?", "What records should I keep?", freelancers setting money aside.

## Approach
1. Tax rules depend on country and year. Use the currency or region in user data; if unclear, ask which country.
2. Explain concepts in general: taxable income, deductions vs credits, advance tax for freelancers, keeping receipts.
3. For rates and limits use web_search for the current year and cite the official source; otherwise say figures may have changed.
4. Offer a simple set-aside habit (e.g. move a percentage of each income into a tax pot) using their income.
5. Legal tax planning only. Never help hide income or evade tax.

## Output
Short explanation, a rough estimate only if the rules are known, and a recommendation to confirm with a qualified tax professional.
