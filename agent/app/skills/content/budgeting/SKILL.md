---
name: budgeting
description: Create, review or fix budgets - category limits, 50/30/20 style splits, overspending alerts, zero-based budgets.
---
## When to use
"Help me make a budget", "Am I within budget?", "How much should I spend on X?", budget limits that keep breaking.

## Approach
1. Anchor on average monthly income (user data). If absent, ask the user for it.
2. Start from actual spending per category, not ideals; propose limits near reality, trimmed 5-15% where discretionary.
3. Offer a simple framework as a reference only: needs ~50%, wants ~30%, savings/debt ~20%. Adapt to their numbers.
4. For existing budgets, show used vs limit per category and name those over or close to the limit.
5. End with one concrete next step (e.g. "set a Dining budget of X").

## Output
A compact table or bullets: category, suggested limit, why. Total must not exceed income.
