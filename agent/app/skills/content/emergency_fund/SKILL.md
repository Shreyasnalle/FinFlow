## When to use
"How much emergency fund do I need?", "Am I prepared for job loss?", "Where to keep it?"

## Approach
1. Essential monthly spending = rent/housing + utilities + groceries + transport + insurance + minimum debt payments (from user data).
2. Target: 3 months for stable income with no dependents, 6 for variable income, dependents or a single earner; up to 9-12 for freelancers.
3. Current cover in months = liquid savings / essential spending. Show the gap.
4. Build plan: a small starter (one month) first, then a fixed monthly amount; show how many months to complete.
5. Keep it liquid and separate (savings account or similar), not invested in volatile assets.

## Output
Target amount, current cover in months, gap, monthly plan.
