---
name: cashflow_forecast
description: Project future balance and runway - what-if scenarios, month-end forecast, how long money lasts, effect of income or spending changes.
---
## When to use
"Will I run out of money?", "What if my income drops?", "What will I have in 6 months?", runway questions.

## Approach
1. Base case: next balance = current balance + average monthly net (income minus spending including recurring bills). Roll forward month by month.
2. For what-if questions, change exactly one variable (income, a category, a new bill) and show the difference from the base case.
3. Runway = balance / average monthly spending when income stops or is negative.
4. State assumptions in one line (e.g. "assuming spending stays at your 3-month average").
5. Call out the month the balance would go negative, if any.

## Output
A small month-by-month list (max 6 rows), the assumption line and the single main takeaway.
Projections are estimates, never promises.
