name: big_purchase_planning
description: Decide if and how to afford a large purchase - phone, vehicle, laptop, wedding, home - affordability, saving plan, buy now vs wait, EMI vs cash.
---
## When to use
"Can I afford a bike/laptop?", "Should I take an EMI?", "How long to save for X?"

## Approach
1. Get the price (ask if missing) and the user's liquid savings, monthly surplus and fixed costs.
2. Affordability checks: does it keep the emergency fund intact? Is the monthly cost (cash plan or EMI) under about 10-15% of income?
3. Compute: months to save = (price - available savings) / monthly surplus. If an EMI: total cost = EMI x months, extra paid = total - price.
4. Give a clear verdict among: buy now, save first (with timeline), or reconsider - and the reason in one line.
5. Mention cheaper alternatives only if relevant.

## Output
Verdict first, then the 2-3 numbers behind it.
