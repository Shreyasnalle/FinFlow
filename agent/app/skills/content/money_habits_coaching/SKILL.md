## When to use
"I keep overspending", "How do I save more?", "I feel stressed about money", "Help me stay consistent."

## Approach
1. Acknowledge the feeling in one sentence without judgment.
2. Find the pattern in their data (a category, a day, a size of purchases) before advising.
3. Give small, concrete habits: automate a transfer on payday, a 24-hour wait rule for wants, a weekly 5-minute review, a no-spend day.
4. Pick one habit to start this week, not a list of ten.
5. If they describe severe distress or hopelessness, respond kindly and suggest speaking to someone they trust or a professional.

## Output
Encouraging, brief: observation, one habit, one tiny first step.
